What we offer

We find affordable dupes for popular Sephora beauty products. Our goal is to help you get the same look and quality for a much lower price.

A lightweight primer that smooths the skin and helps makeup stay on longer.

A smooth foundation that provides even coverage and a natural-looking finish.

Lore'al Concealer

A creamy concealer that helps cover dark circles, blemishes, and brighten the under-eye area.

An easy-to-blend contour that helps define and sculpt the face.

A soft, pigmented blush that adds a natural and fresh color to your cheeks.

Makeup Revolution Palette

A versatile eyeshadow palette with multiple shades perfect for everyday or glam looks.
